Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pᴏɴᴇɢʟʏᴘʜ, a fan-made Discord bot for the One Piece Bounty Rush community, handles information needed to provide its features.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the bot may process or store:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Discord display names.
  • Quiz answers, scores, streaks, and rankings.
  • Achievements and activity statistics.
  • Language preferences.
  • Server settings and leaderboard channels.
  • User-submitted quiz questions and images.
  • Information needed to troubleshoot errors.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate quizzes, calculate scores, display rankings, manage competitions, remember preferences, maintain server settings, and support the bot.

4. Storage

Bot information may be stored in database, configuration, and image files on its hosting service, Silly Development.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The bot operates through Discord and may use external translation services or optional AI services for supported features.

Information sent to these services is subject to their respective privacy practices.

Player names, scores, and rankings may be visible to other Discord users through leaderboards and bot commands.

We do not sell user information.

6. Data Retention

Scores, preferences, submitted content, and server settings may be retained while needed to operate the bot. A fixed automatic deletion schedule has not been established.

Resetting a server leaderboard does not necessarily remove historical global rankings. Data removal requests can be submitted through the support server.

7. Your Choices and Requests

Users may contact the support team to ask about information associated with their Discord account or request its removal.

Requests will be reviewed and handled as reasonably possible and in accordance with applicable law.

8. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect bot information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The bot is intended for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. Users who do not meet those requirements should not use the bot.

10. International Processing

Discord and third-party service providers may process information in different countries, depending on their infrastructure.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the bot or its data practices. The effective date will be updated when changes are published.
